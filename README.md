#SA Tax Practice Assistant.
Is a powerful and simple Tax Addin tool for excel spreadsheets to help staff and user's manage workflowand remember 
important Tax dates before getting penalised by SARS for forgetting, the Dashboards Sidebar keeps pop-up
Banners that have set reminders on all dates from Due dates, Upcoming dates and Alerts for Overdue accounts
that have daily reminder alerts on how many have been missed and will send out Automated E-mails to the Client
aswell as drafting-up pdf E-mail invice and E-mail it to them for billing.
Dates are daily updated while integrated with SARS for Tax Dates and Vat, E-filling, Corporate tax dates
and Individual Tax dates alont with Company registration for all Businessess and Soleprops and UBOs.
this tool was made as simple and User friendly as possible.
just load your client database to sync with your tool and make sure all your data is correctly inputted
and refresh your buttons regularly and the tool does the rest.
